## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-26

### Added

- deterministic `ALLOW / DENY / REQUIRE_APPROVAL / NULL` authorization kernel;
- signed, scoped, expiring, single-use capability grants and request-bound approvals;
- mission, provenance, consequence, blast-radius, and emergency-stop controls;
- protected synthetic adapters with no shell, production API, or real OT path;
- twelve adversarial evaluations and a compromised-model comparator;
- hash-chained, independently verifiable evidence packages;
- unit, integration, property, adversarial, and architecture tests;
- assurance case, threat/control mappings, ADRs, framework crosswalks, and reviewer guide.
